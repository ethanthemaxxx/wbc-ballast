How we work

We write things down. Decisions, incidents and disagreements all get a short document, so a new teammate can read why we did something, not only what.

We keep the team small and the on-call quiet. We run our own product on our own services, and if Ballast pages us, we fix that before we build anything new.

Show the evidence

A claim without a log line, a graph or a diff attached is a guess. Our product holds itself to that, and so do we.

Ask before you ship

Anything that touches production needs a person to approve it. We build that rule into the product and into how we release.

Blame the system

We write postmortems about what failed and what to change, not who was on shift.

Leave it quieter

A good week ends with fewer alerts than it started with.
